Solar System

The Solar System is a gravitationally bound system of the Sun and the objects that orbit it

FACTS ABOUT THE SOLAR SYSTEM

  • The Solar System formed 4.6 billion years ago.
  • The Solar System is located in the Milky Way galaxy.
  • The Solar System consists of the Sun, eight planets, dwarf planets, moons, asteroids, comets, and other celestial objects.
  • The Sun is the largest object in the Solar System, containing more than 99% of its total mass.
